ACTIVITY 2 – CASE STUDY
Title: “University Student Information System Audit” | Time: 30 minutes | 25 points
Case
A university conducts an IT audit of its Student Information System (SIS). The system contains student grades, personal information, enrollment records, and academic histories.
Policy: “Only authorized employees should have access to student records.”
• The system administrator reports that terminated employees are immediately removed.
• The auditor inspects the current user-access list.
• Several accounts belonging to former employees remain active.
• Login logs show that some of these accounts were used after the employees left the university.
• The auditor finds several duplicate user records.
• Management has no documented evidence showing that access reviews are performed regularly.

ACTIVITY 4 – INDIVIDUAL ESSAY
Title: “The Importance of a Systematic IT Audit” | Time: 30 minutes | 30 points
Essay Question
Why is it important to follow a systematic process when conducting an IT audit? Explain how the different audit phases, audit techniques, data analysis, and audit reporting work together to produce reliable audit results.
Instructions
Write 300–400 words. Your essay should discuss:
• The purpose of an IT audit.
• The major phases of an IT audit.
• The importance of collecting reliable audit evidence.
• At least three IT audit techniques.
• The role of audit-data analysis.
• The difference between an anomaly and a confirmed finding.
• The importance of findings, risks, causes, and recommendations.
• The importance of communicating audit results professionally.
